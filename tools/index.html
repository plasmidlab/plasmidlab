Free DNA tools

Seven tools for the checks you make before a cloning experiment. They run in your browser with no account, and nothing needs installing.

  • Restriction site finderEnzymes that cut once, twice or not at all.
  • Restriction enzyme listRecognition sites, overhangs and compatible ends.
  • Virtual agarose gelThe bands your digest will give.

Read a sequence

  • Translate DNA and find ORFsSix reading frames, with the ORFs marked.
  • Reverse complementReverse, complement and reverse complement.
  • GC content and primer TmBase counts, GC% and the Tm of a primer.

Set up a reaction

  • DNA molarity and ligation calculatorng to pmol, and the insert ng for a ratio.

How the numbers are made

The Tm, the fragment sizes and the gel bands come from the PlasmidLab app’s own code, so they match what the app shows for the same sequence. Enzyme sites come from the REBASE list. Your sequence stays in the page, and nothing is uploaded.
