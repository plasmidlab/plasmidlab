A FEW PRACTICAL DETAILS

Before you open it.

Your files, your computer,
your choice of AI.

Do I need an internet connection?

The editor and cloning tools work offline. The AI agent and imports from NCBI or UniProt need an internet connection.

Which AI do I need?

Use a Claude Code, Codex or Grok CLI you already have, or connect an API key of your own. You can use the editor and every cloning tool without an AI provider.

Can I open my existing .dna files?

Yes. PlasmidLab reads and writes .dna, GenBank and FASTA files. Use File > Open, or drop a file on the app window.

Does it work on Intel and Apple silicon?

Yes. PlasmidLab supports both Intel and Apple silicon Macs, and is also available for Windows.

Does the agent send my sequences anywhere?

Only to the AI provider you chose, and only when you ask the agent something.

Where do I send a bug or feature idea?

Email hello@plasmidlab.com, or report a problem or suggest a feature.
