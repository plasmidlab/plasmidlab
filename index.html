06 A few details

Questions
before you install.

Do I need an internet connection?

The editor and cloning tools work offline. The agent and NCBI or UniProt imports need internet.

Which AI do I need?

The free trial gives 3 AI runs per Google account. After that, connect your own Claude, Codex or Grok subscription or API key. The editor and cloning tools need none of them.

Can I open my existing .dna files?

Yes. PlasmidLab reads and writes .dna, GenBank and FASTA. Use File > Open or drop the file on the window. Traces in .ab1 and .scf open too.

Apple silicon or Intel?

Both. PlasmidLab runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, and on Windows.

Does the agent send my sequences anywhere?

Only to the AI provider you chose. Only when you ask the agent something. The free trial goes through PlasmidLab's relay to Anthropic; see the privacy page. The trial is for people aged 18 or older.

Where do I send a bug or a suggestion?

Email support@plasmidlab.com or open an issue on GitHub. Questions go to hello@plasmidlab.com.
