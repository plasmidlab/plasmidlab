05 A few details

Questions before you install.

Do I need an internet connection?

The editor and cloning tools work offline. The AI, BLAST and NCBI, Ensembl or Addgene lookups need internet.

Which AI do I need?

None for the editor. For the AI, you get 10 free runs, then use your own Claude, Codex or Grok plan or API key.

Can I open my existing .dna files?

Yes. PlasmidLab reads and writes .dna, GenBank and FASTA. Traces in .ab1 and .scf open too.

Apple silicon or Intel?

Both. Windows works too.

Does the AI send my sequences anywhere?

Only to the AI provider you chose, and only when you ask it something. Free-trial requests go to Anthropic through PlasmidLab's relay. See the privacy page. The trial is for people 18 and over.

How do I report a bug?

support@plasmidlab.com or GitHub. Questions: hello@plasmidlab.com.
