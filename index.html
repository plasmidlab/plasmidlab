06 A few details

Questions
before you install.

Do I need an internet connection?

The editor and cloning tools work offline. The agent, NCBI BLAST searches and NCBI, Ensembl or Addgene lookups need internet.

Which AI do I need?

10 free runs per account, then your own Claude, Codex or Grok subscription or API key. The editor needs none of them.

Can I open my existing .dna files?

Yes. PlasmidLab reads and writes .dna, GenBank and FASTA. Traces in .ab1 and .scf open too.

Apple silicon or Intel?

Both, and Windows.

Does the agent send my sequences anywhere?

Only to the provider you chose, only when you ask it something. The free trial goes through PlasmidLab's relay to Anthropic; see the privacy page. The trial is for people aged 18 or older.

Where do I send a bug or a suggestion?

support@plasmidlab.com or GitHub. Questions: hello@plasmidlab.com.
