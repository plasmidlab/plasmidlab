About PlasmidLab

PlasmidLab is a plasmid map and sequence editor for Mac and Windows, free to download, with an optional agent that plans a cloning design on your own files.

What it is

The app opens .dna, GenBank, FASTA and EMBL files and .ab1 and .scf sequencing traces, draws the circular and linear map, finds restriction sites, designs primers, and simulates restriction, Gibson, Golden Gate, Gateway and TA/TOPO cloning, PCR, mutagenesis and agarose gels. Files open from and save to your own computer, and the editor works with no connection.

The agent is the part people ask about. You describe the construct you want and it calls the same cloning functions the menus call, on your files, then shows every step, every primer and the product. It does not write sequences itself, so what comes out is an ordinary file you can open and check. It runs on a Claude, ChatGPT or Grok plan you already have, or on a free trial of ten runs.

What it costs

The app is free. The AI is free for ten runs per account, after which it uses a subscription or an API key you already pay for. There is no paid tier of PlasmidLab to upgrade to.

The browser tools

Nine of its functions also run on this site with no account and nothing to install: the free tools. The Tm, the fragment sizes, the gel bands and the dimer energies are computed by the app’s own code, bundled into the page, so a number here matches what the app gives for the same sequence. Restriction sites come from the REBASE list, the same list the app ships. Sequences you paste or drop are worked on in the page and are not uploaded.

Where to find us

Everything the project publishes comes from these accounts:

  • bio.tools, the registry entry.

Questions go to hello@plasmidlab.com, and anything that looks wrong in the software to support@plasmidlab.com or to the issue tracker.

What to expect of it

PlasmidLab is for research use. A design it produces is a design, not an experiment: read the junctions, the reading frame and the feature orientation before you order oligos, and sequence the clone afterwards. The agent can be wrong, and the app says so where it matters.
